How it works

  1. 1

    Your coin moved.

    Say it's down 12% over the last day. That's the number your wallet shows you.

  2. 2

    So did what it's paired with.

    Most coins trade against something else: a stock token, Ethereum or another coin. Here it's NVDA, down 5% in the same hours.

  3. 3

    Here's the split.

    We read both prices from Robinhood Chain for the same start and end time, then split your move: 40% came from NVDA, 60% is the coin's own.
