How it works

  1. 1 / 3

    Your coin moved.

    Say it's down 12% over the last day. That's the number your wallet shows you.

    Your coin 24h
    −12%
    ↓ down 12%

  2. 2 / 3

    So did what it's paired with.

    Most coins trade against something else: a stock token like NVDA, Ethereum, or another coin. If that fell 5%, it dragged your coin down with it.

    Your coin −12%
    Paired with NVDA
    −5%
    ↓ down 5% in the same hours

  3. 3 / 3

    Here's the split.

    We read both prices straight from Robinhood Chain, for the same start and end time, and split your move in two: the part that came from the pair, and the part that's the coin's own.

    You're down 12%. About 40% of that is the stock (NVDA).

    40% the stock (NVDA) 60% the coin itself
